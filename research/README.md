# Research notes

Raw material behind the atlas, kept for transparency and so the sources can be checked.

- [`digest.md`](digest.md): a combined digest of six research passes (Apple platform, shipped consumer apps, builders and open source, research frontier, demand and whitespace), as of September 24, 2026.
- [`raw/`](raw/): the structured findings from each pass, including the walls and policy pass. Each item has a confidence tag (`verified`, `likely`, `uncertain`) and source URLs.

Caveat: many news sites could not be opened during research, so some items rest on search-result snippets. Those are tagged `likely` or `uncertain`. Apple developer documentation claims were checked against developer.apple.com.
