The Line

A phone whose home screen is one conversation. Say or type what you want; the phone plans it as typed capability calls, a fixed policy decides what runs and what asks, and every action lands in a ledger with an undo. A working sketch from the book The Agentic Phone.

What the phone did, and why

The planner only proposes. Each proposed call goes through the Gate, a few lines of fixed code that decide from the capability's effect class, the mode and your grants. The model never approves its own actions.

You→Planner→Capability call→Gate→Run or ask→Ledger + Undo→Card

Try: “Set an alarm for sleep”, “Change the headphone level”, “Connect to Bluetooth”, “Pay Sam $80”. Type / for commands. Shift+Tab changes the mode. Nothing here touches your real device.
